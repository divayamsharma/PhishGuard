What we detect

Built to catch every type of fraud

PhishGuard checks your email against a library of known fraud patterns used by real attackers.

🎣

Phishing & Credential Theft

Detects fake login prompts, "verify your account" requests, password reset scams, and credential harvesting links.

💸

Financial Fraud

Flags wire transfer requests, lottery winnings, Nigerian prince schemes, inheritance funds, and "guaranteed return" traps.

🎭

Impersonation

Identifies fake IRS, Microsoft, Apple, PayPal, Amazon, and government agency notices designed to steal your trust.

⏰

Urgency & Pressure Tactics

Spots high-pressure language like "act now", "expires today", and "account suspended" used to rush victims into mistakes.

🔑

Sensitive Data Requests

Catches direct asks for passwords, PINs, SSNs, bank account numbers, and credit card details including CVV codes.

🔗

Suspicious Links

Detects raw IP address links, shortened URLs (bit.ly, tinyurl), and generic "click here" anchors used to hide destinations.
